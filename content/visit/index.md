## Who we are

Welcome to Long Branch Church. We are a Bible-believing church that exists to bring people into a closer relationship with God. Our mission, vision and aim is to proclaim the true and only Hope that is found in Christ Jesus. We believe and teach that there is only one God, one faith and one Savior.

{{< columns >}}

## Sunday mornings

**Sunday School:** 9:00 AM<br>
**Worship Service:** 10:00 AM

Our worship service is a time of singing together and teaching from God's Word.

{{< column >}}

## Your kids

During the 10:00 worship service we offer:

- **Nursery** for infants to 2 years old
- **Little Kids Church** for Pre-K
- **Big Kids Church** for Kindergarten to 5th grade
